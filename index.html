Who We Are

A Trading House Rooted in Agriculture

Based in Thoothukudi — one of India's historic port cities — we source, consolidate and export the machines that work the land and the produce that comes from it. Our catalogue spans heavy agricultural machinery, precision farm implements and a curated range of fair-trade & bio products.

Every consignment is inspected for export-grade quality, documented end to end, and shipped through established freight partners so it arrives on time, anywhere in the world.

  • Direct sourcing from established manufacturers and growers
  • Complete export documentation & freight coordination
  • Quality inspection before every shipment
  • Long-term partnerships, not one-off transactions
